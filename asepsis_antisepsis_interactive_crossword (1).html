      Quick revision

      Asepsis: measures used to prevent microbial contamination.

      Antisepsis: antimicrobial measures applied to living tissue.

      Sterilization: destruction or removal of all forms of microbial life, including bacterial spores.

      Disinfection: elimination of many pathogenic microorganisms on inanimate objects; it does not necessarily destroy bacterial spores.
